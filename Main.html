About ▶

This website was developed as part of my academic project focusing on Sustainable Development Goal 5: Gender Equality. It aims to educate, inform, and inspire people to take action towards equal rights, opportunities, and respect for all genders.

Issues ▶

1. Discrimination in the workplace

2. Gender-based violence

3. Unequal representation in leadership roles

Solutions ▶

1. Promote equal opportunities and leadership for women.

2. Strengthen laws against harassment and violence.

3. Support inclusive workplaces through awareness and fair policies.

Health Tips ▶

1. Practice self-care and stress management.

2. Maintain a healthy work-life balance.

3. Join support groups for empowerment and confidence.
